Open Pasu FS to check protection

The application files have been installed. Open Pasu FS from Applications to complete any macOS approvals and check the actual protection status. Installing files alone does not confirm that protection is running.

To uninstall, open Pasu FS, go to Overview, and choose “Uninstall Pasu FS…”. Settings and audit records are kept unless you choose to delete them. Protected folders and their files are never uninstall targets.

If macOS requires a restart during removal, restart and open Pasu FS again to finish.
